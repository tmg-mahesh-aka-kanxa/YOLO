🧠 Face Detection Pipeline

The face detection model is powered by a YOLO architecture optimized on a comprehensive Face Detection Dataset sourced from Kaggle. Comprising over 16.7K high-resolution images, this dataset includes a wide variety of human faces captured across diverse real-world constraints—including challenging lighting profiles, severe angles, varying resolutions, and facial occlusions (like glasses and masks). This massive, diverse dataset ensures high precision and robust face tracking in varying real-world environments.

📦 General Object Detection Pipeline

The object detection backbone is built by combining two legendary computer vision benchmarks: PASCAL VOC 2007 and PASCAL VOC 2012. Merging these sets provided a combined training foundation of 21,493 images containing over 52,000 annotated ground-truth objects. The model is trained to accurately segment, localize, and draw bounding boxes around 20 distinct object classes spanning four structural categories:

  • Vehicles: Aeroplane, Bicycle, Boat, Bus, Car, Motorbike, Train
  • Animals / Humans: Bird, Cat, Cow, Dog, Horse, Sheep, Person
  • Indoor Items: Bottle, Chair, Dining Table, Potted Plant, Sofa, TV/Monitor
